Title: LDAUL
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/LDAUL
============================================================
LDAUL = [integer array] Default:
LDAUL = NTYP*2 Description: LDAUL specifies the l -quantum number for which the on-site interaction is added. For
LDAUL =-1 no on-site interaction is added. Mind : one has to specify one number for each atomic species. Related tags and articles LDAU , LDAUTYPE , LDAUU , LDAUJ , LDAUPRINT , LMAXMIX
