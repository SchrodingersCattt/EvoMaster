Title: MSDGW SEED
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/MSDGW_SEED
============================================================
MSDGW_SEED = [integer] Default:
MSDGW_SEED = 2 Description: Seed for pseudo orbitals used by mixed stochastic-deterministic compression algorithm.

Mind:
Available as of VASP 6.6.0
When set in combination with
MSDGW_F
,
MSDGW_SEED changes the random phases of the stochastic orbitals determined by the compression algorithm.
MSDGW_NXI stochastic orbitals
|_1, ·s,|_{N_}
are determined from the original orbitals
|φ_1,·s,|φ_N
by drawing
N_ × N
pseudo numbers
0≤ α_{i n} 1
and forming the linear combinations
|_i = Σ_{n=1}^{N} e^{i 2π α_{in}}|φ_n
.
