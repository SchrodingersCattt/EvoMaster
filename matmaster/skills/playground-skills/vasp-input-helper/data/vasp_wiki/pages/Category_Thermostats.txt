Title: Category:Thermostats
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/Category:Thermostats
============================================================
Thermostats
are used in
molecular-dynamics calculations
within the
NVT ensemble
and
NpT ensemble
in order to apply a certain temperature to the ionic degrees of freedom.
Choose between stochastic
thermostats
:
Andersen thermostat
Langevin thermostat
CSVR thermostat
and deterministic
thermostats
:
Nosé-Hoover thermostat
Nosé-Hoover chain thermostat

Mind:
All
thermostats
are available in the
NVT ensemble
but currently only the
Langevin thermostat
is available for the
NpT ensemble
.

ensembles
