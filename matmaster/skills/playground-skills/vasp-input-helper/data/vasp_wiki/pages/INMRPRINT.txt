Title: INMRPRINT
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/INMRPRINT
============================================================
INMRPRINT = -1 | 0 | 1 Default:
INMRPRINT = 0 Description: Determines the verbosity of the NMR output. Mind: Available as of VASP.6.6.0. The NMR output is written to the OUTCAR . Setting INMRPRINT 0 is the default, while 1 prints more, and -1 prints less. Related tags and articles LCHIMAG , LNMRLEG
