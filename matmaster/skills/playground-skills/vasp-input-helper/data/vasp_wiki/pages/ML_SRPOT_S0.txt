ML_SRPOT_S0 = [real] Default:
ML_SRPOT_S0 = 2.0 Description: Specifies the broadening of a soft repulsive exponential potential used as an auxiliary potential in thermodynamic integration ( VCAIMAGES ). In most use cases a value between 1 and 2 is optimal.
