Title: DQ
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/DQ
============================================================
DQ = [real] Default:
DQ = 0.001 Description: step size for the finite difference k -space derivative in the linear response calculation of chemical shifts. Typical values for DQ are in the range [0.001 - 0.003]. The default is often sufficient. Related tags and articles LCHIMAG , ICHIBARE , LNMR_SYM_RED , NLSPLINE
