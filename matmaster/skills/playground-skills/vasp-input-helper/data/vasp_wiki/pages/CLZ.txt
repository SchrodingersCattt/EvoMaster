CLZ = [real] Default:
CLZ = 0 Description: CLZ selects a fractional or the full electron charge for the core hole in the core-excitation calculations with
ICORELEVEL =2. In SCH can be used to introduce a fraction of the core hole. For example, a half-core hole can be created to simulate a transition state in the X-ray excitation process. Related tags and articles ICORELEVEL , CLNT , CLN , CLL , LHARTREE , NBANDSV , NBANDSO , OMEGAMAX , ANTIRES
