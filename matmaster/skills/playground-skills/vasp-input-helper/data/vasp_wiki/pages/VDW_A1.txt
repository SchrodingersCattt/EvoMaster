Title: VDW_A1
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/VDW_A1
============================================================
VDW_A1 = [real] Description: VDW_A1 sets the scaling a_{1} of the critical radii in the rational Becke-Johnson damping function of the DFT-D3 and DFT-D4 methods. VDW_A1 allows to set the scaling a_{1} of the critical radii in the rational Becke-Johnson damping function: DFT-D3 (
IVDW =12). simple-DFT-D3 (
IVDW =15 with
SDFTD3_DAMPING =rational, mrational, or optimizedpower). DFT-D4 (
IVDW =13). Related tags and articles IVDW , VDW_A2 , DFT-D3 , simple-DFT-D3 , DFT-D4
