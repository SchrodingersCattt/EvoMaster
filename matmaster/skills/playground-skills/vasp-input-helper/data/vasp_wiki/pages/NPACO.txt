Title: NPACO
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/NPACO
============================================================
NPACO = [integer] Default:
NPACO = 256 Description: NPACO sets the number of slots in the pair-correlation function written to PCDAT . VASP evaluates the pair-correlation function each NBLOCK steps and writes the PC-function after NBLOCK × KBLOCK steps to the PCDAT file. Related tags and articles APACO , NBLOCK , KBLOCK , PCDAT
