Title: PHON_NEDOS
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/PHON_NEDOS
============================================================
PHON_NEDOS = [integer] Default:
PHON_NEDOS = 2000 Description: Sets the number of frequency points to compute the phonon density of states. The density of states is computed between [ω_{{min}}-5σ,ω_{{max}}+5σ] with ω_{{min}} and ω_{{max}} the lowest and highest phonon frequency and σ the broadening PHON_SIGMA . Mind: Only available as of VASP 6.4.0. Related tags and articles QPOINTS , PHON_NWRITE , LPHON_POLAR , PHON_DIELECTRIC , PHON_BORN_CHARGES , PHON_G_CUTOFF
