Title: EFERMI_NEDOS
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/EFERMI_NEDOS
============================================================
EFERMI_NEDOS = [integer] Default:
EFERMI_NEDOS = 21 Description: Number of Gauss–Legendre integration points used to evaluate the Fermi–Dirac distribution and determine the Fermi level at finite temperature using the tetrahedron method only with
ISMEAR = −14 or -15 . Mind: Available as of VASP 6.5.0 EFERMI_NEDOS sets the number of points in the Gauss–Legendre grid used to integrate the Fermi–Dirac distribution for determining the Fermi level within the tetrahedron method when
ISMEAR = −14 or -15 . Larger values improve accuracy, especially at low temperatures or with sharp features in the electronic DOS , but also increase computational cost. A brief convergence test is recommended in case very accurate occupancies are required, e.g., in the context of transport calculations . Mind: ELPH_FERMI_NEDOS is a valid alternative way of writing this tag. Implementation details At T=0 , the integrated and differential densities of states are $$ n(ε)=Σ_{n{k}}(ε-ε_{n{k}}), g(ε)=Σ_{n{k}}δ(ε-ε_{n{k}}). $$ At finite temperature, $$ N_e(ε_F,T)= Σ_{n{k}}f(ε_{n{k}}-ε_F,T) =∫ g(ε)f(ε-ε_F,T)\,dε. 1 $$ With the substitution x = 1 - 2f(ε-ε_F,T) , $$ ε = k_BT\!{1+x}{1-x}+ε_F, dε = -k_BT\,{2}{x^2-1}\,dx, $$ Eq. (1) becomes $$ N_e(ε_F,T)= {1}{2}∫_{-1}^{1} n\!(k_BT\!{1+x}{1-x}+ε_F)\,dx. $$ In practice, this integral is discretized as $$ N_e(ε_F,T) {1}{2}Σ_{i=1}^{N}w_i\, n\!(k_BT\!{1+x_i}{1-x_i}+ε_F), $$ where w_i and x_i are Gauss–Legendre weights and abscissas. The step functions \((ε-ε_{n{k}})\) entering \(n(ε)\) are evaluated using the tetrahedron method, with the number of energy points N given by EFERMI_NEDOS .
