Title: LVTOT
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/LVTOT
============================================================
LVTOT = [logical] Default:
LVTOT = .FALSE. Description: Determines whether the total local potential V_{{LOCPOT}}({r}) (in eV) is written to the LOCPOT file. V_{{LOCPOT}}({r}) = V_{{ionic}}({r}) + ∫ {n({r'})}{|{r}-{r'}|}d{r'}+ V_{{xc}}({r}) where V_{{ionic}}({r}) is the ionic potential, the second term is the Hartree potential, and V_{{xc}}({r}) is the (semi-)local exchange-correlation potential. If
LVTOT =.TRUE., the V_{{LOCPOT}}({r}) is written to the LOCPOT file and the POT file. The POT file additionally contains the augmentation part. Warning:
LVHAR =T changes the content of the LOCPOT file. WRT_POTENTIAL also gives access to the total local potential and offers more options. Related tags and articles Computing the work function , LVHAR , LOCPOT , WRT_POTENTIAL , LVACPOTAV , POT
