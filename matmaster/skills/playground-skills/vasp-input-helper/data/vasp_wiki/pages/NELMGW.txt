Title: NELMGW
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/NELMGW
============================================================
NELMGW = [integer] Default:
NELMGW = 1 Description: NELMGW sets the number of self-consistent GW steps. Available as of 6.3.0. This tag is effective for
ALGO =EVGW | QPGW | GW[R][K] and ignored otherwise. For instance
ALGO = EVGW0
NELMGW = 4 performs a partially self-consistent GW calculations , where G is updated four times. Omit NBANDS and NELM to select the single-step GW procedure . Related tags and articles ALGO , GW calculations
