Title: IVDW_NL
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/IVDW_NL
============================================================
IVDW_NL = [integer] Default:
IVDW_NL = 1 for a
GGA = 2 for a METAGGA Description: IVDW_NL allows to select the kernel of the nonlocal van der Waals part of a functional (available as of VASP.6.4.0).
IVDW_NL =1 corresponds to the kernel of Dion et al.

and
IVDW_NL =2 to the kernel rVV10

. Note that the kernel of Dion
et al.
contains one adjustable parameter (
ZAB_VDW
), while the rVV10 kernel contains two such parameters (
BPARAM and
CPARAM
).
