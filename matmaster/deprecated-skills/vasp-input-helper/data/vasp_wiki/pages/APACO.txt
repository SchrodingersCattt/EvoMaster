Title: APACO
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/APACO
============================================================
APACO = [real] Default:
APACO = 10 Description: APACO sets the maximum distance in the evaluation of the pair-correlation function (in Å). VASP evaluates the pair-correlation function each NBLOCK steps and writes it after NBLOCK × KBLOCK steps to the PCDAT file. Related tags and articles NPACO , NBLOCK , KBLOCK , PCDAT
