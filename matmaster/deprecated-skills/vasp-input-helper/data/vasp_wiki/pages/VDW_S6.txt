Title: VDW_S6
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/VDW_S6
============================================================
VDW_S6 = [real] Description: VDW_S6 sets a parameter in the damping function of van der Waals methods. VDW_S6 allows to set the value of a parameter in the following methods: Scaling s_{6} of the dipole-dipole dispersion of the DFT-D2 (
IVDW =1), DFT-D3, DFT-D4 (
IVDW =13), DFT-ulg (
IVDW =3), and Tkatchenko-Scheffler methods (
IVDW =2 and 21). VDW_S6 can be used for both implementations of DFT-D3: DFT-D3 (
IVDW =11 or 12) and simple-DFT-D3 (
IVDW =15). Steepness factor a_0 in DDsC (
IVDW =4). Mind: The setting of s_{6} with VDW_S6 when
IVDW =11 or 12 (VASP implementation of DFT-D3 ) is possible since VASP.6.6.0. Related tags and articles IVDW , VDW_S8 , DFT-D2 , DFT-D3 , simple-DFT-D3 , DFT-D4 , DFT-ulg , Tkatchenko-Scheffler method , Tkatchenko-Scheffler method with iterative Hirshfeld partitioning , DDsC
