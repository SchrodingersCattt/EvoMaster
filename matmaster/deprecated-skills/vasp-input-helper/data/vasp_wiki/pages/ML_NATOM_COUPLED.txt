ML_NATOM_COUPLED = 0 Description: This tag specifies the number of atoms for which a coupling parameter is introduced to calculate the chemical potential within the machine learning force field method. For thermodynamic integration calculations please have a look at the tag ML_LCOUPLE . Related tags and articles ML_LMLFF , ML_LCOUPLE , ML_ICOUPLE , ML_RCOUPLE
