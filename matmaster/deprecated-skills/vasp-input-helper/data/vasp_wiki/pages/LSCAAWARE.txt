Title: LSCAAWARE
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/LSCAAWARE
============================================================
LSCAAWARE = [logical] Default:
LSCAAWARE = .TRUE. if VASP is compiled with scaLAPACK support (precompiler flag -DscaLAPACK) = .FALSE. otherwise Description: LSCAAWARE controls the distribution of the Hamilton matrix. For
LSCAAWARE =.TRUE., VASP distributes the Hamilton matrix among the MPI ranks. For
LSCAAWARE =.FALSE., each MPI ranks allocates the complete Hamiltonain. In both cases LSCALAPACK decides if ScaLAPACK routines are used for diagonalization. Related tags and articles NPAR , NCORE , LPLANE , NSIM , KPAR , LSCALU , LSCALAPACK
