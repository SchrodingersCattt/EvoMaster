Title: LWRT_AUGMENTED_DENSITY
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/LWRT_AUGMENTED_DENSITY
============================================================
LWRT_AUGMENTED_DENSITY = .TRUE. | .FALSE. Default:
LWRT_AUGMENTED_DENSITY = .TRUE. Description: Switch for WRT_DENSITY to write without augmentation (compensation charge=0). With
LWRT_AUGMENTED_DENSITY = F the densities can be written without augmentation (compensation charge=0). Mind that the augmented densities are still used during electronic minimization to evaluate the XC functional (unlike for the MGGA specific tag LNOAUGXC ). Related tags and articles WRT_DENSITY , ENCUT , NGXF , NGYF , NGZF Workflows that use this tag
