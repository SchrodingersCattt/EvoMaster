Title: KPOINT_BSE
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/KPOINT_BSE
============================================================
KPOINT_BSE = [integer] (optionally [integer],[integer],[integer]) Description: KPOINT_BSE specifies the k-point index at which VASP calculates the dielectric matrix. In the simplest form, one can specify KPOINT_BSE = index_of_k-point Select the desired k point from the list of k points in the OUTCAR file. Additionally, a shift by an arbitrary reciprocal lattice vector can be supplied by specifying three additional integer numbers: KPOINT_BSE = index_of_k-point n1 n2 n3 This allows calculating the dielectric function at a k point outside of the first Brillouin zone corresponding to {k} + n_{1} {b}_{1}+ n_{2} {b}_{2} + n_{3} {b}_{3} where {b}_{i} are the reciprocal-lattice vectors of the unit cell. Warning: We strongly recommend using
ANTIRES =2 for the finite wavevector calculations. The Tamm-Dancoff approximation can lead to unphysical results for the dielectric function at a finite wavevector. Related tags and articles BSE calculations
