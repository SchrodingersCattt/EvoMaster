Title: LIBXC2_Pn
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/LIBXC2_Pn
============================================================
LIBXC2_Pn = [real]

Description:
LIBXC2_Pn
, where
n=1, 2,
allows to specify the values of the parameters of the functional implemented in Libxc that is called with
LIBXC2
.
A detailed explanation on how to use
LIBXC1_Pn
and
LIBXC2_Pn
and an example can be found at the
LIBXC1_Pn
page.
