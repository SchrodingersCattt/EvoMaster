Title: Blocked-Davidson algorithm
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/Blocked-Davidson_algorithm
============================================================
The workflow of the blocked-Davidson iterative matrix diagonalization scheme implemented in VASP is as follows:

Take a subset (block) of
n_1
orbitals out of the total set of
NBANDS orbitals:
\{ _n| n=1,..,N_{ bands}\} \{ ^1_k| k=1,..,n_1\}
.
Extend the subspace spanned by
\{^1\}
by adding the preconditioned residual vectors of
\{^1\}
:
\{ ^1_k \, / \, g^1_k = (1- Σ_{n=1}^{N_{ bands}} | _n _n | { S} ) { K} ({ H} - ε_{ app} { S} ) ^1_k \, | \, k=1,..,n_1 \}.
Rayleigh-Ritz optimization ("subspace rotation") within the
2n_1
-dimensional space spanned by
\{^1/g^1\}
, to determine the
n_1
lowest eigenvectors:
{ diag}\{^1/g^1\} \{ ^2_k| k=1,..,n_1\}
Extend the subspace with the residuals of
\{^2\}
:
\{ ^2_k \,/ \, g^1_k \, / \, g^2_k = (1- Σ_{n=1}^{N_{ bands}} | _n _n | { S} ) { K} ({ H} - ε_{ app} { S} ) ^2_k \, | \, k=1,..,n_1 \}.
Rayleigh-Ritz optimization ("subspace rotation") within the
3n_1
-dimensional space spanned by
\{^1/g^1/g^2\}
:
{ diag}\{^1/g^1/g^2\} \{ ^3_k| k=1,..,n_1\}
If need be the subspace may be extended by repetition of this cycle of adding residual vectors and Rayleigh-Ritz optimization of the resulting subspace:
{ diag}\{^1/g^1/g^2/../g^{d-1}\} \{ ^d_k| k=1,..,n_1\}
Per default VASP will not iterate deeper than
d=4
, though it may break off even sooner when certain criteria that measure the convergence of the orbitals have been met.
When the iteration is finished, store the optimized block of orbitals back into the set:
\{ ^d_k| k=1,..,n_1\} \{ _k| k=1,..,N_{ bands}\}
.
Move on to the next block
\{ ^1_k| k=n_1+1,..,2 n_1\}
.
When
LDIAG =.TRUE. (default), a Rayleigh-Ritz optimization in the complete subspace \{ _k| k=1,..,N_{ bands}\} is performed after all orbitals have been optimized. The blocksize n_1 used in the blocked-Davidson algorithm can be set by means of the NSIM tag. In principle n_1= 2× NSIM , but for technical reasons it needs to be dividable by an integer N : n_1 = { int}({2*{ NSIM} + N - 1}{N}) N where N is the "number of band groups per k-point group": N = {{ \#\; of\; MPI\; ranks}}{{ IMAGES}*{ KPAR}*{ NCORE}} (see the section on parallelization basics ). As mentioned before, the optimization of a block of orbitals is stopped when either the maximum iteration depth ( NRMM ), or a certain convergence threshold has been reached. The latter may be fine-tuned by means of the EBREAK , DEPER , and WEIMIN tags. Note: we do not recommend you to do so! Rather rely on the defaults instead. The blocked-Davidson algorithm is approximately a factor of 1.5-2 slower than the RMM-DIIS , but more robust. References ↑ G. Kresse and J. Furthmüller, Comp. Mater. Sci. 6 , 15 (1996) ↑ G. Kresse and J. Furthmüller, Phys. Rev. B 54 , 11169 (1996).
