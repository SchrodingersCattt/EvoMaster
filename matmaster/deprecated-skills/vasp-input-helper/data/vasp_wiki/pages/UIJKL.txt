Title: UIJKL
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/UIJKL
============================================================
The
UIJKL file stores the effectively screened Coulomb integrals at the unit-cell origin ( R = 0):
U_{ijkl}^{σσ'} = ∫ { d}{ r}∫ { d}{ r}'
w_{i}^{*σ}({ r}) w_{j}^{σ}({ r}) U({ r},{ r}',ω)
w_{k}^{*σ'}({ r}') w_{l}^{σ'}({ r}')
evaluated at zero frequency (
ω=0
). The columns I, J, K, L are the Wannier function indices; RE and IM are the real and imaginary parts. The format is as follows:
# U_ijkl = [ij,R|kl,0]
# I J K L RE(V_IJKL) IM(V_IJKL)
1 1 1 1 4.3457689208 0.0000000000
2 1 1 1 0.0000021313 0.0000001349
...
