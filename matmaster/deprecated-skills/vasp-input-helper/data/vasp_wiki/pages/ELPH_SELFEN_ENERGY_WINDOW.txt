Title: ELPH_SELFEN_ENERGY_WINDOW
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/ELPH_SELFEN_ENERGY_WINDOW
============================================================
ELPH_SELFEN_ENERGY_WINDOW = [real, real] Default:
ELPH_SELFEN_ENERGY_WINDOW = 0.0 0.0 Description: Specifies the energy window (in eV) around the band edges within which the electron-phonon self-energy is computed. Mind: Available as of VASP 6.5.0 The self-energy is evaluated for electronic states with energies in the intervals around the valence band minimum (VBM) and the conduction band minimum (CDM), with an energy window defined with
ELPH_SELFEN_ENERGY_WINDOW = a b : from VBM – a up to VBM, and from CBM up to CBM + b Related tags and articles ELPH_RUN
