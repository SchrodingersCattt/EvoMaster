Title: IEPSILON
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/IEPSILON
============================================================
IEPSILON = 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 Description: IEPSILON determines along which Cartesian direction the E field is applied.
IEPSILON =1 x direction
IEPSILON =2 y direction
IEPSILON =3 z direction
IEPSILON =4 x, y and z direction; three indpendent calculations are performed Important: IEPSILON only applies to the time-propagation algorithm. Related tags and articles BSE calculations
