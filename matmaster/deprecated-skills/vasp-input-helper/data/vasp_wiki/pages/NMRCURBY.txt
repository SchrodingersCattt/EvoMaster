Title: NMRCURBX
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/NMRCURBY
============================================================
Mind:
Available as of VASP 6.6.0
The
NMRCURBX file contains the NMR current density in atomic units (hartree bohr
^2
). It is written if
WRT_NMRCUR is set. The format is the same as
CHGCAR with a header to define the grid and then 3 blocks that correspond to
j_x
,
j_y
, and
j_z
.
