Title: ELPH_SELFEN_G_SKIP
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/ELPH_SELFEN_G_SKIP
============================================================
ELPH_SELFEN_G_SKIP = [logical] Default:
ELPH_SELFEN_G_SKIP = .FALSE. Description: Skip the computation of the electron-phonon matrix elements and instead assume their numerical value is 1. Mind: Available as of VASP 6.5.0 This option is intended for debugging purposes, as it allows testing the self-energy and transport routines without performing the full evaluation of the coupling matrix elements.
