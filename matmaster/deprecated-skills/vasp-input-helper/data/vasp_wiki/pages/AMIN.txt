AMIN = [real] Default:
AMIN = min(0.1, AMIX , AMIX_MAG ) Description: AMIN specifies the minimal mixing parameter in Kerker's initial approximation

to the charge-dielectric function used in the Broyden

/Pulay

mixing scheme (
IMIX =4,
INIMIX =1). Kerker's initial approximation

for the charge-dielectric function is given by
({AG^2}{G^2+B^2},A_{ min}),
where A
=
AMIX
, B
=
BMIX
, and
A_{ min}
=
AMIN
.
