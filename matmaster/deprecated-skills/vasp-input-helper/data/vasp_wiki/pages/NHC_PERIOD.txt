Title: NHC_PERIOD
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/NHC_PERIOD
============================================================
NHC_PERIOD = [real] Default:
NHC_PERIOD = 40 Description: Time scale of the Nosé-Hoover chain in terms of the number of MD steps. NHC_PERIOD sets the time scale (

) of the
Nosé-Hoover chain thermostat
. It is expressed in terms of the number of MD steps and must be interpreted in combination with the time step set by
POTIM
. The setting
NHC_PERIOD =0 corresponds will generate the NVE ensemble. Tip: The value of NHC_PERIOD should correspond to a characteristic time scale in the system. If this is unknown, NHC_PERIOD should be set to a value between 20 and 200. Related tags and articles POTIM , NHC_NCHAINS , Nosé-Hoover chain thermostat
