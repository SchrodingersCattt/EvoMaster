============================================================
The
constrained random-phase approximation
(cRPA) is a method that allows the calculation of the effective interaction parameter U
, J
, and
J'
for model Hamiltonians.
The main idea is to neglect the screening effects of specific target states in the screened Coulomb interaction W
of the
GW method
.
The resulting partially screened Coulomb interaction is evaluated in a
localized basis
that spans the target space and is described by the model Hamiltonian.
The target space is usually low-dimensional and therefore allows for the application of a higher-level theory, such as dynamical-mean-field theory (DMFT).
More information about cRPA is found on the following page:
