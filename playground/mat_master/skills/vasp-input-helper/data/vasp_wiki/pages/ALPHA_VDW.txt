Title: ALPHA_VDW
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/ALPHA_VDW
============================================================
ALPHA_VDW = [real] Default:
ALPHA_VDW = 0.94950 for
IVDW_NL =3 or = 0.28248 for
IVDW_NL =4 Description: Specify α in vdW-DF3-opt1/vdW-DF3-opt2. The ALPHA_VDW tag allows to specify the value of the parameter α in the kernel of the vdW-DF3-opt1/vdW-DF3-opt2 nonlocal van der Waals functionals.
