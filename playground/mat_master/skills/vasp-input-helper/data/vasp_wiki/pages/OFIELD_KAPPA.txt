OFIELD_KAPPA = [real] Description: The tag OFIELD_KAPPA sets the strength of the bias potential in the Interface pinning method. The bias potential in the Interface pinning method is written as U_{bias}({R}) = 2 (Q_6({R}) - A)^2 . The tag OFIELD_KAPPA method sets the strength of the bias potential

. The unit of

rm{eV}/(rm{unit} \,\, rm{ of }\,\, Q)^2
