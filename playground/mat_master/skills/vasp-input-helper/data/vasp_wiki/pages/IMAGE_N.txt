IMAGE_N

Description: The
IMAGE_N allows defining the
INCAR settings for calculations using
images
.
IMAGE_N can define the
INCAR settings used for a calculation in an image calculation, e.g.,
01
,
02
for
nudged elastic band
calculations,
parallel tempering
, and
thermodynamic integration
.
