Title: ML_LEMPPOT
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/ML_LEMPPOT
============================================================
ML_LEMPPOT = [logical] Default:
ML_LEMPPOT = .FALSE. Description: Enables the use of auxiliary empirical potentials for thermodynamic integration using VCAIMAGES .
ML_LEMPPOT =.TRUE. sets the following defaults:
ML_LCOUPLE =.TRUE.
ML_RCOUPLE =0.0
ML_LEMPPOT =.TRUE. requires
ML_LCOUPLE =.TRUE., so no other values for ML_LCOUPLE are permitted. Related tags and articles ML_LMLFF , ML_LCOUPLE , ML_ICOUPLE , ML_RCOUPLE , ML_NATOM_COUPLED , ML_EMPPOT_RCUT , ML_SRPOT_B0 , ML_SRPOT_N0 , ML_SRPOT_S0 , ML_MOPOT_NM , ML_MOPOT_DM , ML_MOPOT_RM , ML_MOPOT_RKM , ML_MOPOT_IJM
