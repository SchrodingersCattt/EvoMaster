Title: Category:Wannier functions
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/Wannier_states
============================================================
Wannier functions
|w_{m{R}}
are constructed by a linear combination of Bloch states
|_{n{k}}
, i.e., the computed Kohn-Sham (KS) orbitals, as follows:
|w_{m{R}} =
Σ_{n{k}}
e^{-i{k}·{R}}
U_{mn{k}}
|_{n{k}}.
Here,
U_{mn{k}}
is a unitary matrix which can be generated using different approaches discussed below, m
is an index enumerating Wannier functions with position
{R}
, n
is the band index, and
{k}
is the Bloch vector.
Generally, one starts with an initial guess for
U_{mn{k}}
that is built from
A_{mn{k}}
. The latter can be built from projections onto some localized-orbital basis.
Comprehensive instructions on
how to construct Wannier orbitals
.
One-shot singular-value decomposition (SVD)
In one-shot SVD,
A_{mn{k}}
is computed by projecting the KS orbitals onto localized orbitals basis
φ_{m{k}}
that is specified by the
LOCPROJ tag:
A_{mn{k}} =
_{n{k}} | S |φ_{m{k}},
where
φ_{i{k}}({r}) = e^{{i}{k}·{r}} Y_{lm}(r)R_n(r).
Note that i
encodes the quantum numbers n
, l
, and m
. Thus, in
A_{mn{k}}
, m
is not the magnetic quantum number.
Then, VASP performs one-shot SVD for each k point
A_{mn{k}} = [D V^*]_{mn{k}}
to obtain the unitary matrix
U_{mn{k}} = [DV^*]_{mn{k}}.
Selected columns of the density matrix (SCDM)
The SCDM method

is switched on using
LSCDM
. It has the advantage that the specification of a local basis in terms of atomic quantum numbers is omitted.
Maximally localized Wannier functions using Wannier90
The interface of VASP with the Wannier90 code

is mainly controlled by
LWANNIER90 and
LWANNIER90_RUN
. First, the initial guess for
A_{mn{k}}
can be created by providing the
projections block
in the
wannier90.win
file (also see
WANNIER90_WIN
) and setting
LWANNIER90 =True. In order to obtain maximally localized Wannier functions, U_{mn{k}} is constructed in a second step. For this, A_{mn{k}} could be created using any projection method in the first step, i.e., single-shot SVD method ( LOCPROJ ), SCDM method ( LSCDM ), or Wannier90 ( LWANNIER90 ). Then, Wannier90 can be executed directly or through VASP with the LWANNIER90_RUN tag. References ↑ A. Damle and L. Lin, Multiscale Model. Simul., 16(3) , 1392–1410 (2018). ↑ A. A. Mostofi, J. R.
