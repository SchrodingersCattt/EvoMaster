Title: CSVR_PERIOD
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/CSVR_PERIOD
============================================================
CSVR_PERIOD = [real] Default:
CSVR_PERIOD = 0 Description: Time scale of the CSVR thermostat in terms of the number of MD steps. CSVR_PERIOD sets the time scale (

) of the
CSVR thermostat
. It is expressed in terms of the number of MD steps and must be interpreted in combination with the time step set by
POTIM
. Typically,
CSVR_PERIOD should take the values corresponding to 2-2000 fs, whereby the smaller the value, the more aggressive the thermostating. The special setting
CSVR_PERIOD =0 generates the NVE ensemble. Related tags and articles POTIM , CSVR thermostat
