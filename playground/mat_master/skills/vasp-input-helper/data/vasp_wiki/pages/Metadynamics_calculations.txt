Title: Metadynamics calculations
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/Metadynamics_calculations
============================================================
Anderson thermostat
For a metadynamics run with Andersen thermostat, one has to:
Set the standard MD-related tags:
IBRION =0, TEBEG , POTIM , and NSW Set
MDALGO =1 (
MDALGO =11 in VASP 5.x), and choose an appropriate setting for ANDERSEN_PROB Set the parameters HILLS_H , HILLS_W , and HILLS_BIN Define collective variables in the ICONST -file, and set the STATUS parameter for the collective variables to 5 If needed, define the bias potential in the PENALTYPOT -file The actual time-dependent bias potential is written to the HILLSPOT -file, which is updated after adding a new Gaussian. At the beginning of the simulation, VASP attempts to read the initial bias potential from the PENALTYPOT -file. For the continuation of a metadynamics run, copy HILLSPOT to PENALTYPOT . The values of all collective variables for each MD step are listed in REPORT -file, check the lines after the string Metadynamics .
