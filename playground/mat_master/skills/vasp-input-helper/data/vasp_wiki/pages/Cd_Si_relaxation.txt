Break of symmetry in standard diamond structure: change z position from 0.125 to 0.130.

System = diamond Si
ISTART = 0 ;

ISMEAR = 0;

KPOINTS k-points 0 Monkhorst Pack 11 11 11 0 0 0 Calculation Example output after 10 relaxation steps: POSITION TOTAL-FORCE (eV/Angst) ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 4.81253 4.81253 4.81250 -0.000724 -0.000724 -0.000031 0.68747 0.68747 0.68750 0.000724 0.000724 0.000031 ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- total drift: 0.000000 0.000000 0.000000 Files to watch during relaxations: stdout (terminal): each electronic step is written to the terminal: OSZICAR : a copy of the terminal output. OUTCAR : more detailed information on every electronic and ionic step. Other important files: CONTCAR : holds the structure of the last ionic step and at the end the structural result (also very important for restarting a relaxation). STOPCAR : stops a relaxation.
