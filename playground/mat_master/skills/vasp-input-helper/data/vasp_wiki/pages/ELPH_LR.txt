Title: ELPH_LR
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/ELPH_LR
============================================================
ELPH_LR = [integer] Default:
ELPH_LR = 1 Description: Controls the treatment of the long-range part of the electron-phonon potential. Mind: Available as of VASP 6.5.0 This tag controls the treatment of the long-range electrostatic contributions to the electron-phonon coupling arising in polar dielectric materials. Mind: In this case, the required Born effective charges and dielectric tensor are read from the phelel_params.hdf5 file. Modes ELPH_LR ≤ 0 No long-range correction scheme is applied to the electron-phonon coupling. This is most likely very inaccurate for semiconductors and insulators with non-vanishing Born effective charge.
ELPH_LR = 1 Dipole corrections are applied to the electron-phonon coupling
