Title: vaspwave.h5
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/Vaspwave.h5
============================================================
The
vaspwave.h5
file is the hdf5 counterpart of the
WAVECAR and
CHGCAR and other files used inside a more elaborate workflow, like for instance GW.
This file is written if
LH5 =.TRUE. is set in the INCAR file. Mind: As of version 6.6.0 this files stores optionally following datasets. energies and orbitals from WAVECAR charge density from CHGCAR optical transition elements from WAVEDER Wannier projection matrices WANPROJ local potential POT Related tags and articles LCHARGH5 , LH5 , WAVECAR , CHGCAR , WAVEDER , WANPROJ , POT
