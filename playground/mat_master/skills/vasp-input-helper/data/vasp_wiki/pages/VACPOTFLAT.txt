Title: VACPOTFLAT
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/VACPOTFLAT
============================================================
VACPOTFLAT = [real] Default:
VACPOTFLAT = 0.1 Description: Maximum permissible 2D-averaged electric field for a region considered to be field-free in eV/Å. A region of space is considered to be field-free if the 2D-averaged electric field (
LVACPOTAV =True) is smaller than VACPOTFLAT . Tip: Increase VACPOTFLAT for a quick estimation of the vacuum potential and decrease for a precise value. If the cell is large and EDIFF small, the final result of LVACPOTAV should be independent of VACPOTFLAT .
