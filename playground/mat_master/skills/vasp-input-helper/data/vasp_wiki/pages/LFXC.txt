Title: LFXC
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/LFXC
============================================================
LFXC = .TRUE. | .FALSE. Default:
LFXC = .FALSE. Description: LFXC enables the local exchange-correlation kernel in TD-DFT and TDHF calculations. The local exchange-correlation kernel is determined via

f^{ loc}_{{xc}}({r}, {r}') =
{δ^{2}\{E_{{c}}^{{DFT}}+(1-c_{ x})
E_{{x}}^{{DFT}}\}}{δ ({r}) δ
({r}')}
,
where
c_{ x}
is the fraction of the exchange interaction set by
AEXX
.
