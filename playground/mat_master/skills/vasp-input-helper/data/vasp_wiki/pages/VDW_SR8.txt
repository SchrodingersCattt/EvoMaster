Title: VDW_SR8
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/VDW_SR8
============================================================
VDW_SR8 = [real] Description: VDW_SR8 sets the radii scaling s_{r,8} in the dipole-quadrupole zero-damping function of the DFT-D3 method. Mind: Available as of VASP 6.6.0 VDW_SR8 allows to set the radii scaling s_{r,8} in the dipole-quadrupole zero-damping function of the DFT-D3 method. VDW_SR8 can be used for both implementations of DFT-D3: DFT-D3 (
IVDW =11) and simple-DFT-D3 (
IVDW =15 with
SDFTD3_DAMPING =zero or mzero). Related tags and articles IVDW , VDW_SR , DFT-D3 , simple-DFT-D3
