Title: LWAVEH5
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/LWAVEH5
============================================================
LWAVEH5 = [logical] Default:
LWAVEH5 = LH5 Description: Determines whether the wavefunctions are written to vaspwave.h5 file at the end of a run. If
LWAVEH5 = True , the Kohn-Sham orbitals, i.e., wavefunctions, are written to the vaspwave.h5 file at the end of the calculation. This can be used to restart VASP. Deprecated: LWAVEH5 is deprecated as of VASP 6.4.3 and is not read/used by the code. It was introduced in VASP 6.1.0. Related tags and articles LCHARG , LCHARGH5 , LWAVE , LH5
