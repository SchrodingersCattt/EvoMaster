Title: ML_EMPPOT_RCUT
URL: https://vasp.at/wiki/ML_EMPPOT_RCUT
============================================================
ML_EMPPOT_RCUT = [real] Default:
ML_EMPPOT_RCUT = 0.0 Description: Sets the cutoff radius for the empirical potentials. The empirical potentials have a separate neighbor list. ML_EMPPOT_RCUT not only determines the cutoffs of the empirical potential functions but also the cutoff of the neighbor list.
